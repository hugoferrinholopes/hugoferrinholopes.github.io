Hugo Ferrinho Lopes

  • FLAD Visiting Professor, Department of Government, Georgetown University

I study how people act within democratic institutions and how their actions change those institutions in return. My work follows voters, party members and young people as they decide whom to support, when to push back, and how to make their way into politics.
